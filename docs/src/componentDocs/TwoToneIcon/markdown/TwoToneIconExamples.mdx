## Two Tone Icon

`TwoToneIcon` applies a semantic two-color palette to a two-tone icon. Pass an icon to `icon` and choose its color with `status`. It is available from `@brightlayer-ui/icons-mui`.

### Icon Colors

Use `status` to communicate meaning:

- **Primary:** Default actions and navigation.
- **Error:** Errors, failures, and destructive actions.
- **Warning:** Cautionary messages and attention-required states.
- **Success:** Confirmations and positive states.
- **Orange:** High-priority or maintenance states.
- **Purple:** Specialized or branded features.
- **Neutral:** Inactive, disabled, or low-emphasis icons.
